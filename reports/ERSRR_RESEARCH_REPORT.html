06 / paper posture

What the evidence permits.

The strongest paper is currently a rigorous spatial-transfer and false-alarm tradeoff study, not a model-superiority claim. Published MARS-S2L numbers remain contextual because their cohort is not this paired strict cohort.

Supported

  • ERSRR materially lowers same-cohort false-positive rate.
  • The recall loss is large, statistically stable, and deployment-relevant.
  • Failures concentrate in transferable representation, with seed-unstable false positives.
  • The evaluation is reproducible, leakage-aware, and uncertainty-quantified.

Not supported

  • ERSRR does not outperform released MARS-S2L overall.
  • Published MARS-S2L test results are not a paired comparison.
  • Post-hoc strata do not authorize threshold changes.
  • External positive-domain confirmation is not complete until CDS wind is acquired.

Representation first

Pretrain or distill against released MARS-S2L features; preserve sensitivity before optimizing abstention.

Hard strata

Design acquisition around medium plumes, low/high wind, long reference intervals, and geography.

Calibrated cascade

Separate high-recall proposal generation from quality-aware false-alarm rejection.

New untouched cohort

Preregister v4 hypotheses and evaluate exactly once on spatial groups never used here.
