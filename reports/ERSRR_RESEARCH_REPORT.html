01 · Executive decision

Architecture follows label quality.

The research does not support scaling the legacy Vision Transformer. It supports a compact, segmentation-first core with a strict artifact contract—while the project invests primarily in physically meaningful labels, temporal pairs, negatives, and leakage-proof evaluation.

Adopt the 144,433-parameter, five-channel raw residual U-Net as the shared research core, label it research_only, and keep regression disabled until physical-unit calibration no longer depends on the target. Physics features and bitemporal L2A remain experiments, not selected architecture.

Why this core

Small enough to diagnose

It trains quickly on CPU, preserves spatial structure, handles explicit nodata masks, and uses only the five declared bands. The clean ablation does not justify expanding the serving contract with physics-derived channels.

What changes

One train/serve contract

Canonical band order, feature transforms, train-only normalization, masked losses, model construction, artifact metadata, and SHA-256 validation belong in one shared core.

What stays blocked

No physical regression claim

The legacy predictor uses the held-out EMIT target percentiles to denormalize outputs. Until a target-independent calibration exists, only segmentation is scientifically defensible.

02 · Computer-use acquisition

A real V002 pilot, with provenance.

NASA Earthdata Search was inspected interactively, a genuinely new EMIT V002 plume granule was selected, and the public metadata path was turned into a repeatable acquisition pipeline. Eleven more granules were then collected in parallel.

Workflow executed

Select an unseen V002 plume

EMIT_L2B_CH4PLM_002_20250922T204933_003374, near −102.047°, 32.241° in the Permian region.

Capture authoritative metadata

Query NASA CMR for the exact granule; store UMM JSON, physical plume polygon GeoJSON, browse image, source URLs, timestamps, and hashes.

Pair public Sentinel-2 L2A

Search Element84 Earth Search within ±14 days and <20% cloud; choose bracketing acquisitions and crop B2/B3/B4/B11/B12 COGs.

Force one geospatial contract

Reproject both timestamps and the physical polygon to a shared 20 m, 256 × 256 grid. This caught and fixed a cross-UTM-zone mismatch in the initial pair.

Scale and validate

Run the collector across 12 granules, then verify every declared file, SHA-256, grid, and before/after mask footprint.

03 · Dataset and leakage audit

The bottleneck is measurable.

The existing 102-tile dataset is internally consistent in shape but not in label validity, temporal alignment, or evaluation design. These defects are large enough to dominate architectural differences.

Label semantics

Every tile is forced positive

The legacy segmentation code labels each tile’s top 10% of methane values. That is a relative rank, not a physical plume boundary, and it cannot represent a true negative.

Evaluation leakage

Target used at inference

Standalone inference denormalizes predictions using percentile statistics from the held-out EMIT target. Physical-unit performance derived this way is circular.

Serving mismatch

Legacy training ≠ production

The pre-refactor server reversed the canonical visible-band order, normalized each tile differently, and loaded a different checkpoint. The shared artifact contract now prevents those mismatches; its output remains a research probability mask, not a calibrated methane quantity.

What did not work
01

Physics expansion was not selected

Raw ResU-Net beats physics ResU-Net at both thresholds; single-time raw and physics logistic are effectively tied on V002.

02

The U-Net did not beat simple ranking

Raw logistic AUPRC exceeds raw ResU-Net at >300 (0.4481 vs 0.4168) and >1000 ppm·m (0.1243 vs 0.0953).

03

V002 cannot adjudicate temporal input

Six polygons are clipped, only six complete groups remain, and nested folds train on just three or four groups.

Interpretation boundary

These numbers answer a narrower question

  • They test leakage-aware ranking and segmentation on the data currently available.
  • They do not establish calibrated methane concentration or flux.
  • They do not validate geographic transfer or detection on true negative scenes.
  • The V002 masks are plume-complex polygons, not per-pixel enhancement rasters.
  • The same EMIT-time polygon on pre/post S2 cannot distinguish transient methane from persistent context.
  • Legacy L1C and new L2A observations must remain separate domains unless harmonization is explicit.
Useful result: the clean experiments narrow the supported architecture to a five-band raw research baseline. Complete labels, contemporaneous controls, negatives, and independent groups—not extra channels—are the next investment.

06 · Best path forward

A data-first research sequence.

Each gate is designed to prevent compute from hiding a data or evaluation defect. A larger foundation backbone is deliberately deferred until the benchmark demonstrates learnable methane signal.

NEXT · 01

Build the negative class

Add at least 200 geographically and seasonally matched plume-free L2A pairs. Validate absence using known-plume catalogs and explicit valid masks.

Gate: AUPRC above prevalence
NEXT · 02

Expand physical V002 masks

Acquire protected EMIT V002 COGs through an authorized Earthdata session, grow to ≥50 geographically held-out validation groups, and keep temporal offsets tight.

Gate: stable 5-fold intervals
NEXT · 03

Test bitemporal input

Collect contemporaneous labels or explicit no-plume controls before stacking pre/post L2A observations. Compare against MBMP and single-time controls under identical grouped folds.

Gate: beat single-time baseline
NEXT · 04

Inject physics-shaped plumes

Add controlled synthetic methane absorption to plume-free SWIR scenes using flux/wind buckets, then fine-tune on real V002 masks and test by region.

Gate: real-only holdout gain
Only after the gates

Consider a pretrained Earth-observation backbone

Prithvi-EO-2.0, SatMAE, or another multispectral encoder may help once the physical-mask benchmark has enough independent positives and negatives. Until then, a bigger model mostly increases variance and hides failure modes.

Publication-grade endpoint

Detection, then calibrated quantification

Report per-region precision/recall, IoU, AUPRC, detection-vs-flux curves, repeated-seed intervals, and a frozen external holdout. Add IME-to-flux only after target-independent concentration calibration and wind uncertainty are validated.
