05 / MARS-S2L context

Three comparisons.
Only one is paired.

MethaneS2CM is the paired v5.1 test. The MARS strict cohort compares v4.3 and the released checkpoint. Published MARS tables remain different-cohort context and cannot be used as paired evidence.

Supported

On MethaneS2CM, v5.1’s AP, AUROC, recall, pixel AP, Dice, and IoU are higher than released MARS-S2L, and the group bootstrap supports the AP/AUROC/recall/overlap directions.

Not supported

V5.1 has not yet beaten MARS-S2L on every no-plume and plume criterion, nor has it been evaluated on MARS’s official test under an identical train/product contract.

06 / paper contract

Publish the advance.
Keep the boundary.

The strongest defensible paper is a spatial-transfer, calibrated-risk, and dataset-contract study. The current result is substantial enough to report, but the superiority claim remains conditional on a new confirmation cycle.
