# MARS sensor-aware ordinal reporting contract

This sidecar was frozen while the native folds-3/4 continuation was still in
training and before any held-fold prediction, comparator semantic decode, gate,
or final outcome existed. It does not modify the running model or scientific
protocol.

The future descriptive verifier must report absolute candidate and comparator
AP, fold/sensor AP, the full five-point matched-FPR recall curve, aggregate
dense TP/FP/FN and IoU, and the existing paired-site uncertainty. Every delta
must independently reproduce the immutable compact result to `1e-12`. It may
not select a threshold, weight, checkpoint, seed, subgroup, blend, or report
variant.

The final successor HTML is a new artifact. The historical v5.1 dossier at
`reports/ERSRR_RESEARCH_REPORT.html` remains unchanged. MARS-S2L paper-v3
Tables S5/S6 and the slightly stronger reconstructed archive are included as
official-benchmark context, but development folds 3/4 are explicitly a
different cohort. Passing the development gate cannot be described as official
paper superiority.

The machine-readable authority is
`configs/mars_sensor_ordinal_reporting_protocol.json`.
